# Sanity reviews

_Owner: sanity-agent. Lock this file before you change it (see `RULES.md`); the lock name is
`sanity-reviews.md`._

Per review, newest first: date, what was reviewed, the verdict (In customer interest, Concerns,
Not in customer interest), the check against the project boundaries, and the reasons. A scope
review judges every task, decision and requirement added since the last one (within the
requirements, outside them, or not needed) and ends with the line
`Reviewed up to: T-060, D-036, REQ-027`, from which the check counts the new items.
Compaction keeps the latest review per subject in full and shortens older ones to one line.
