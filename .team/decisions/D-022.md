## Decision

One `IExceptionHandler` registered with `AddProblemDetails()` and
`UseExceptionHandler()` turns every error into RFC 9457 Problem Details: validation 400 (with
`errors`), not found 404, business rule 422, concurrency conflict 409, unexpected 500 without
stack trace or internal text outside Development, always with `traceId`. The MVC
`DomainExceptionFilter` is removed. Every endpoint takes a `CancellationToken` and passes it to
services, EF Core and PDF generation; `OperationCanceledException` after the client aborted is
logged at Debug, not as an error, and gets no 500 (status 499 in logs only).

## Why

Task 13 found two error paths (`DomainExceptionFilter` plus `UseExceptionHandler`)
and no `CancellationToken` anywhere in controllers or services. Impact: API only; 3 story
points (B5), tester adds checks for each status code (T2, 1 story point).
