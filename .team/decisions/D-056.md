## Decision

1. **Numbers:** `yyyy-nnnn`, the next number = highest of that year + 1, computed inside the create transaction as today; the unique index on `Invoice.Number` and the existing retry (3 attempts) handle two invoices at once. No PostgreSQL sequence. Replaces "invoice numbers from a sequence" in D-024 (the rest of D-024 stays).
2. **Delete (Q-083 c):** only an open invoice that is the most recent one (the highest number of the latest year, so the last one issued) can be deleted; any other gives the business-rule error (400, as all business rules since Q-063) with the reason "Only the most recent unpaid invoice can be deleted", localized (D-050). Its lines become uninvoiced again, in one transaction, as now. The next invoice gets the freed number again, so there is no gap. REQ-024 criterion 1 then reads "…is refused, except the most recent unpaid invoice (Q-083)": the orchestrator puts that wording to the user (change after approval).
3. **Term (BUG-6, Q-028): as D-057** (amended 2026-11-20 17:40; the first version removed `paymentTermDays` from the request). Due date = invoice date + 14 days, always; the request field stays and any other value gets the localized business-rule 400 (built in 17596d4, T-054). The invoice screen shows the term as read-only text and sends 14 (F10 in build-tasks.md).
Details and pieces: architecture/build-tasks.md, S4.

## Why

A PostgreSQL sequence is not transactional: a rolled-back or failed invoice still uses its number, so it gives gaps, which the S4 goal and Q-083 forbid; MAX+1 in the transaction is gapless and fast at about 100 owners. Rejected: a counter row per year locked with `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` (also gapless, but a new table and migration for no gain at this volume). Delete: rejected deleting any open invoice (gaps) and no delete at all (the user chose c). Term: D-057 is built and tested (496 green) without a contract change; removing the field would mean rework in the API, Logic, Contracts and tests for no user-visible gain, since the screen hides the field anyway.
