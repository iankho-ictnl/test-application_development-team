## Decision

As in Database architecture (task 2): separate `propertyadmin_migrator` and
`propertyadmin_app` database roles; production migrations as a separate step, not at API
start-up; `xmin` row version on the changeable entities with 409 on conflict; invoice numbers
from a sequence; partial index for uninvoiced lines and `Invoice(OwnerId, Status, DueDate)`;
paging on every list; daily `pg_dump` with quarterly restore test.

Updated 2026-11-09 (T-026) with the user's answers: volumes at most 100 owners, 200 assets
and 10,000 activities per year (Q-033, REQ-022), so one PostgreSQL instance on the same host
is ample and the indexes above suffice. RPO 24 hours and RTO 2 days (Q-037 replaces RPO 0 from
Q-033; REQ-023): a daily `pg_dump` meets it, so no standby server and no WAL archiving. The
backup keeps 14 daily and 12 monthly dumps on a backup volume separate from the database
volume; a copy off the host depends on Q-039 (no off-host storage today). Invoices are kept
at least 7 years (Q-034, REQ-024): no hard delete of invoices or owners; anonymization of
inactive owners afterwards waits for Q-041.
Updated 2026-11-10 (T-041), superseding the unresolved details above: the expected scale is 100 owners, 200 assets, 10,000 activities and up to 10,000 expenses per year, with lists and reports under 2 seconds (Q-033, Q-040, REQ-022). One PostgreSQL instance and the listed indexes suffice. RPO is 24 hours and RTO is 2 days (Q-037); keep 14 daily and 12 monthly dumps on a backup volume separate from the database volume but on the same host. The user accepts that losing the host also loses all backups; no off-host copy is in the current scope (Q-039). Hosting is capped at 1,000 euro per year, including server, domain and backup storage (Q-042, REQ-027); maintenance has a separate cap of 100 euro per month (Q-046), counting only paid services and checked per calendar month (Q-048). Invoices and lines are retained at least 7 years from invoice date; anonymization is proposed by the system only after seven years from the owner being marked inactive, and requires Admin confirmation (Q-034, Q-041, Q-044, REQ-024).

## Why

Today the API connects as the PostgreSQL superuser and migrates at start-up, and no
entity has a concurrency token. Impact: migration, compose init script, API paging; 5 story
points (B6 3, U8 2). Rejected for recovery: streaming replication to a synchronous standby
(needed for RPO 0, but no second machine, Q-037); continuous WAL archiving (no off-host
storage, so it would not survive the loss of the host either).

Accepted by the user, 2026-11-09.
