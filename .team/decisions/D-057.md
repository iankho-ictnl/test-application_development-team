## Decision

`InvoiceService.PaymentTermDays = 14` (BR-11, Q-028): the due date is always invoice date + 14 days. `POST /api/v1/invoices` (and `/api/invoices`) still takes `paymentTermDays`, but any value other than 14 is refused with a business-rule 400, key "The payment term is 14 days." (nl: "De betalingstermijn is 14 dagen.", D-050). Suggested frontend follow-up (frontend developer, architect decides): show the 14 days read-only instead of an editable 0..365 field.

## Why

Removing the field would break the frontend contract (frontend not ours to change). Silently ignoring another value would show the user a term that is not used. Refusing it is honest and keeps the contract.
