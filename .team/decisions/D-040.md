## Decision

The proxy publishes `${HTTP_PORT:-80}:80` and `443:443`. Production leaves `HTTP_PORT` empty (80). The laptop sets `HTTP_PORT=8080`: `http://localhost:8080` redirects (308) to `https://localhost`. Still only the proxy publishes ports (REQ-025, D-023).

## Why

T-070: Windows on the laptop reserves TCP port 80 (`netsh interface ipv4 show excludedportrange protocol=tcp` lists 80-80), so `docker compose up` failed at the proxy ("bind: forbidden by its access permissions"). Rejected: removing the Windows reservation (admin change to the user's machine, not ours to make); a compose override per environment (D-039 rejects that); making 443 configurable too (Caddy's redirect then points to the wrong port, and 443 is free).
