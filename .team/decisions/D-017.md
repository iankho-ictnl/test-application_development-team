## Decision

Upgrade PDFsharp-MigraDoc from 6.1.1 to 6.2.4, the newest version under the MIT
license (it supports .NET 10). Do not take 6.5.0 until the user has answered question 21.

## Why

this deviates from "newest version" on purpose. 6.5.0 (2026-10-14) changed the license
to AGPL-3.0 only (GNU Affero General Public License), with a paid commercial license as the
alternative, and code must now select the license. Decision #9 chose PDFsharp because it was MIT,
and REQ-16 allows no extra budget. The AGPL generally requires that the source code of the whole
application be offered to the people who use it over a network; whether that is acceptable is the
customer's choice, and may need legal advice. The vendor does not say whether 6.2.x still gets
fixes; that is a risk, and the way forward (accept the AGPL, buy a license, or replace the library
with another free one in a new decision) depends on question 21.
