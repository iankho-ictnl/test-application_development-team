## Decision

QRCoder 1.6.0 to 1.8.1; Microsoft.VisualStudio.Azure.Containers.Tools.Targets
1.24.2-preview.1 to 1.23.0.

## Why

newest stable versions. A preview is not allowed, and 1.24.2-preview.1 is unlisted on
NuGet. QRCoder 1.7 and 1.8 list no breaking change for `QRCodeGenerator` and `PngByteQRCode`,
which the profile page uses. If the "Container (Dockerfile)" launch profile in Visual Studio 2026
does not work with 1.23.0, the frontend developer raises an open question; no preview is used.
