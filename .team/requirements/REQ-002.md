## Requirement

Manage owners.

## Acceptance criteria

The analyst's US-2.1 to US-2.4 in analysis.md (from the user's answers Q-025 and Q-026), recorded 2026-11-10 (Q-050):
- US-2.1: Given staff signed in, when they save a new owner with name, address, email and phone, then the owner appears in the owner list; when one of these is missing, saving is refused with a message.
- US-2.2: Given an existing owner, when staff change and save the owner's data, then the list and the owner's assets show the new data.
- US-2.3: Given an owner that still has assets, when staff try to delete the owner, then it is refused with a message, because every asset must keep exactly one owner (BR-1).
- US-2.4: Given any owner (with or without assets or invoices), when staff want to remove the owner, then the owner is never deleted, only marked inactive, and its invoices stay available (retention and anonymization: REQ-024).

Changed by the user's answers Q-087 (2026-11-11; recorded in T-074; design D-041) and Q-088 and Q-089 (2026-11-11; recorded in T-075). These criteria make "address" in US-2.1 concrete; the user approves them again together with D-041:
- US-2.1a: The owner's address is five separate fields: street, house number, postal code, city and country. Each is required; saving an owner with one of them empty is refused with a message at that field.
- US-2.1b: An owner has an optional KvK number and an optional BTW (VAT) number (owners can be private persons); saving without them succeeds. When given, a KvK number is exactly 8 digits and a BTW number is any EU VAT number format: one of the 27 EU member-state prefixes (Greece `EL`) followed by 2 to 12 letters or digits, and for `NL` the form NL123456789B01 (spaces and dots ignored); otherwise saving is refused with a message at that field (Q-088, 2026-11-11: foreign VAT numbers occur). For example DE123456789 and BE0123456789 are accepted, XX123 and NL123 are refused.
- US-2.1c: The invoice PDF shows the owner's name, street and house number, postal code and city, country, and the KvK and BTW number when they are filled in, always as the owner's current details: after a change of the owner, a PDF of an older invoice shows the new details too (Q-089, 2026-11-11: no copy on the invoice date).
- US-2.1d: Foreign addresses are accepted (Q-088): an owner with a country other than the Netherlands and a postal code in another format (for example "1000" for Brussels) can be saved; the address fields are only checked as filled in.
