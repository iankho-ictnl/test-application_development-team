## Requirement

Manage assets (building/home, address, owner).

## Acceptance criteria

The analyst's US-3.1 to US-3.4 in analysis.md (from the user's answers Q-025 and Q-027), recorded 2026-11-10 (Q-050):
- US-3.1: Given an existing owner, when staff save a new asset with type building or home, an address and that owner, then the asset appears in the asset list with its owner.
- US-3.2: Given a new or changed asset without an owner, when staff save it, then it is refused (BR-1).
- US-3.3: Given an asset form, when staff choose the type, then only building and home can be chosen; when staff save an asset without the full address (every address part, including country), then saving is refused with a message.
- US-3.4: Given an asset with uninvoiced and invoiced activities or expenses, when staff change its owner, then the uninvoiced lines move with the asset to the new owner (offered on the new owner's next invoice), and the invoiced lines and their invoices stay with the old owner.

Changed by the user's answer Q-087 (2026-11-11; recorded in T-074; design D-041). This criterion makes "the full address" in US-3.3 concrete:
- US-3.3a: The asset's address is the same five separate fields as the owner's: street, house number, postal code, city and country. Each is required; saving an asset with one of them empty is refused with a message at that field; the asset list shows the address.
