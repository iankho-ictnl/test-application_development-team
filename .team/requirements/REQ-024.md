## Requirement

Invoices and their lines are kept at least 7 years from their invoice date. An inactive owner
becomes eligible for anonymization seven years after the date they are marked inactive (Q-044),
provided all of their invoices have also been retained for at least 7 years. The system proposes
eligible owners; an Admin must confirm each anonymization (Q-041).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given an invoice younger than 7 years, when any user or process tries to delete it or its
  lines, then this is refused, except the most recent unpaid invoice (Q-083; D-056; wording
  approved by the user 2026-11-20 18:06) (no delete endpoint for sent invoices; the database role of the
  API cannot bypass it through the application).
- Given an active owner, an owner marked inactive less than 7 years ago, or an owner with an
  invoice younger than 7 years, when anonymization is attempted, then it is refused with 409.
- Given an owner who meets all retention conditions, when the system checks for candidates,
  then it proposes that owner and does not anonymize them automatically.
- Given an eligible owner, when an Admin confirms the proposal, then the owner's name, email,
  phone and address, and the addresses of their assets, are replaced by fixed placeholders;
  invoice amounts, numbers and dates remain unchanged, and the change cannot be undone.
