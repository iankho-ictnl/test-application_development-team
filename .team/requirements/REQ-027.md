## Requirement

Hosting costs at most 1,000 euro per year, including the server, domain name and backup storage
(Q-042). Separately, maintenance costs at most 100 euro per month (Q-046); this cap is not part
of the 1,000 euro hosting cap. Only paid services count as maintenance (licences and external
services); hours spent are not included, and the cap is checked per calendar month, not averaged
over the year (Q-048).

## Acceptance criteria

- Given the production hosting costs for a full year, when the server, domain name and backup
	storage costs are totaled, then the total is no more than 1,000 euro.
- Given the invoices of any single calendar month for paid services (licences and external
	services) used to run or maintain the system, excluding the hosting items above and excluding
	hours spent, when they are totaled, then the total is no more than 100 euro.
