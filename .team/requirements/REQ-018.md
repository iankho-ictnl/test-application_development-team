## Requirement

Money as `decimal` (numeric(18,2)), EUR, rounding half away from zero per line.

## Acceptance criteria

Given an activity of 1.5 h x EUR 33.33 at 21% VAT, when it is invoiced, then the line is EUR 50.00 excl. (49.995 rounded half away from zero), VAT EUR 10.50; amounts are `numeric(18,2)` in the database and `decimal` in code (no `double`/`float`); invoice totals equal the sum of the rounded lines.
