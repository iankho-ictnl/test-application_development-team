## Requirement

The system handles at least 100 owners, 200 assets and 10,000 activities per year, plus up to
10,000 expenses per year (Q-040). Lists and reports respond in under 2 seconds. Activity and
expense records are retained for at least 7 years (REQ-024).

How the first criterion is verified (Q-052, no change to the criteria): once, on the production
server before go-live, with only the fictitious seven-year data set and no real data.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a database at the expected seven-year scale (100 owners, 200 assets, and up to 70,000
  activities and 70,000 expenses), when a list or report is run on the production host, then
  it responds in under 2 seconds.
- Given that database, when any list endpoint is called, then it returns one page (default 50,
  maximum 200) sorted on a stable key, and never the whole table.
- Given that database, when the uninvoiced lines of one owner, open invoices of one owner, or
  activities or expenses of one asset in one period are queried, then the query plan uses the
  indexes listed in Database architecture rather than sequentially scanning Activity, Expense
  or Invoice.
