## Requirement

Overview per owner with open invoices.

## Acceptance criteria

The analyst's US-9.1 to US-9.5 in analysis.md (from the user's answer Q-028), recorded 2026-11-10 (Q-050):
- US-9.1: Given owners with invoices that are Open and Paid, when staff open the overview, then per owner only the Open invoices are shown, with number, date and total incl. VAT.
- US-9.2: Given the overview, then the open amount per owner equals the sum of the totals of that owner's Open invoices.
- US-9.3: Given an Open invoice, when staff mark it Paid, then it no longer appears in the overview and the owner's open amount drops by its total.
- US-9.4: Given an Open invoice, then the overview shows its due date as invoice date + 14 days; given today is after the due date, then the invoice is marked overdue.
- US-9.5: Given an owner without Open invoices, when staff open the overview, then that owner is not listed.
