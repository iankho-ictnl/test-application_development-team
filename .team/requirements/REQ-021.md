## Requirement

Dutch/European date and number formatting (nl-NL culture).

## Acceptance criteria

Given the Dutch UI, when dates and amounts are shown or entered, then dates are `dd-MM-yyyy` and amounts `€ 1.234,56`; input `1.234,56` is accepted as 1234.56; PDF invoices use the same format.
